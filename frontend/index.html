An assistant that
keeps you moving

Live Mode is a conversation, not a dictation box. Anything you must do yourself, it points out.

  1. 01

    One answer, several fields

    “I’m Asha, my email is asha@example.com.” Both are noted, and each is read back for your yes.

  2. 02

    Options, the way you say them

    “The second one”, “option B”, or just “I’m a student”, in your own language. Tap the option if you prefer.

  3. 03

    Correct as you go

    “Actually, change my email.” AwaazForm goes back to that question and checks the new answer with you.

  4. 04

    Catch and fix

    Answers are checked as you speak. If one won’t fit, you hear why in plain words and try again.

  5. 05

    Speak your language

    English and 12 Indian languages, including mixed speech like Hinglish. Names are written in English letters for English forms.

  6. 06

    Talk, type, or both

    Live Mode listens hands-free and you can interrupt it. Typing and tapping work at every step.

Know what happens
to your answers

Responsible by design, and enforced in code rather than promised in a policy.

  • ✓ Never submits

    There is no code path that presses Submit. You always submit the form yourself.

  • ✓ Nothing filled without your yes

    Every answer is read back. Only confirmed answers reach the form.

  • ✓ Every action visible

    A timeline shows each step the AI takes, in plain language, as it happens.

  • ✓ No passwords or payments

    Passwords, OTPs, CAPTCHAs, card and bank details are never filled.

  • ✓ Your data stays with you

    Saved details live in your browser only, with optional passphrase encryption.

  • ✓ Audio is never stored

    Your voice is sent for transcription and discarded. AwaazForm keeps no recordings.
