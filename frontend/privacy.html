Privacy

In short: your details stay in your browser. The only thing that leaves it is what is needed to understand your voice and reply.

Stored in your browser only

  • Profiles and saved details (name, email, phone, date of birth, address), only after you said yes to saving each one. Kept in IndexedDB until you delete them on the Profiles page.
  • The current session (questions and answers), kept in local storage so a reload does not lose your work. Use “Discard session” to clear it.
  • Uploaded PDFs and Word files, kept in IndexedDB so a reload doesn’t lose them, until you discard the session.
  • Settings such as language and contrast.

Never saved

  • Passwords, PINs, OTPs, card or bank account numbers.
  • Government ID numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driving licence, voter ID).
  • Health information, religion, caste, sexual orientation, political views, immigration status.
  • Audio recordings. Audio is discarded after transcription and is never written to disk.

Sent to Groq, and why

  • When you answer by voice: the short audio clip, to be transcribed.
  • When AI help is used: the current question, a list of the form’s questions and options (never your answers to them), and what you just said. Your full profile is never sent. A saved detail is sent only after you agree to use it.
  • For read-back, if server text-to-speech is configured: the sentence being read aloud. Otherwise your device’s own voice is used and nothing is sent.
  • For printed PDFs: the page text, to find the questions.
  • Groq’s own retention rules apply to what it receives. Check Groq’s privacy policy before real-world use.

How your data is protected

  • Passkeys: lock saved details with a passphrase, then unlock with your fingerprint, face or security key. The secret comes from your device’s secure hardware (WebAuthn PRF) and never leaves it.
  • Encryption: saved details use AES-256-GCM with a random key that is itself encrypted by your passphrase or passkey. Answers and files kept for a session are encrypted with a key that can’t be read out of this browser.
  • Privacy shield: emails, phone, Aadhaar and PAN numbers are replaced with placeholders before text goes to the AI, and restored on your device.
  • Short memory: session answers and files are deleted after 24 hours without use; unlocked details lock after 10 minutes idle.
  • No tracking: AwaazForm collects no analytics at all, so there is no usage data to protect or anonymise.

Our server

  • Form links: when you paste one, the server fetches that public Google or Microsoft form to read its questions. Your answers never pass through the server; they go into a pre-filled link that opens in your own browser.
  • The server only relays requests to Groq. It stores nothing and does not log request bodies.
  • The Groq API key lives only on the server and is never sent to your browser or the extension.
  • The server only answers this website and the AwaazForm extension, so other sites can’t use it.

Live Mode

  • Live Mode is off by default and asks first. While it is on, it sends your voice and the current question to Groq.

Browser extension

  • The extension reads the questions on the page you open it on, and types an answer into that page only after you confirm it. It never presses Submit. On sites other than Google and Microsoft Forms it asks for permission first.

How to delete everything

  • Profiles page → “Forget everything”. Session page → “Discard session”. Or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
