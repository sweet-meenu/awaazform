Browser extension · Chrome and Edge

Fill any form
right on the page

Bolu sits in the corner of the form you’re on. Talk to it, check each answer, and it types your confirmed answers into the real form. It never presses Submit.

Download the extension
  1. 1

    Unzip it

    Open the downloaded file and extract the awaazform-extension folder somewhere you’ll keep it.

  2. 2

    Add it to your browser

    Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions), turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and pick the folder.

  3. 3

    Open a form

    On a Google Form or Microsoft Form, Bolu appears in the bottom corner. On any other site, click the AwaazForm button in the toolbar.

  4. 4

    Allow the microphone once

    The first time, tap Allow microphone in the widget and press Allow. You can also just type.

Good to know

  • The widget uses this AwaazForm server for voice and AI. You can change it in the widget’s settings.
  • Forms with several pages: answer what’s on this page, press Next on the form, then tap Read again.
  • On sites other than Google and Microsoft Forms, the extension asks for permission to read that one site first.
  • Answers in the widget are kept only until you close the browser.
